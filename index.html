Style Guide & Preparation

Dress Code & What to Bring

Dress code: Smart casual, with a touch of gold. Here are three simple tips to get ready for the evening.

Tip 01

Smart Casual with a Touch of Gold

Polished blazers, tailored trousers, neat collared shirts, chic jumpsuits, or cocktail dresses. Add your signature touch of gold—a warm metallic watch, jewelry, tie, belt buckle, brooch, or shimmering footwear.

Tip 02

Bring a Rooftop Evening Layer

While Level 12 features a sheltered, heated lounge pavilion, the open skyline deck gets a brisk night breeze after sundown. We recommend bringing a stylish blazer, wrap, shawl, or light jacket so you can comfortably enjoy the view.

Tip 03

Just Your Festive Spirit

Leave your work laptops, notebooks, and stress at your desk! Food, beverages, entertainment, photo prints, and celebration gifts are entirely provided. Just bring your team spirit, appetite, and camaraderie.
